About the hotel

A home for pilgrims in Al Hajlah

Shrook Al Misk — “the dawn of musk” — is named for the two things we care about most: early mornings that begin with Fajr, and rooms that always feel fresh when you return to them.

We are a family-run hotel in the Al Hajlah district of Makkah, serving Umrah and Hajj guests, families, and groups. Our promise is simple: honest prices, spotless rooms, and a front desk that understands the schedule of worship — because we live by it too.

Amenities

Haram transport

Help arranging shuttle and taxi rides to Masjid al-Haram, timed around the prayers.

24-hour front desk

Late flight? Early Fajr departure? Check in and get help at any hour, in Arabic, English and Urdu.

Daily housekeeping

Fresh linens, clean bathrooms, and rooms reset while you are at the Haram.

Qibla markers in every room, prayer mats on request, and prayer times displayed in the lobby.
