Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

At TAQWASTEPS, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, where it is kept and who can see it when you use the TAQWASTEPS app for Android, the TAQWASTEPS web app and this website.

TAQWASTEPS has no adverts and no tracking or analytics tools. We do not sell your information.

1. Information We Collect

These are saved to your TAQWASTEPS account on our server:

  • Account Information — Your name, email address, password and date of birth when you create an account. Your password is kept by our login service in a scrambled form that we cannot read. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google gives us your name and email address and we receive no password.
  • Your Salah log — Which prayers you marked, on which day, and how (in jamaat, on your own, qaza). It is saved so that the app and the web app show the same history.
  • Scoreboard details, only if you join the scoreboard — Your name as it is on your account, the country you choose, your Salah, Tahajjud and Quran streaks, your friends and your friend requests.
  • Web app reminders — If you enter your masjid's times in the web app, they are saved with your reminder choices and the address your browser gives us for sending you notifications.

These reach us only when you send them:

  • Messages — Bug reports, feedback, emails and WhatsApp messages. A bug report sent from the app goes through your own email app, so we see your email address, your message, the app version, your Android version and your phone model.
  • Waitlist — Your email address, if you join the waitlist on this website.

Payment Information — TAQWASTEPS is free while it is being tested and takes no payments. If Premium subscriptions begin, payments will be processed by Google Play. We will not receive or store your card details.

2. What Stays on Your Device

Everything else stays on your phone, or in your browser if you use the web app. It is not sent to us:

  • Dhikr counts and daily routines
  • Quran progress and bookmarks
  • Your Tahajjud log and Tahajjud reflections
  • The fasting tracker
  • The reason you give when you pause your streaks
  • Your profile picture, which only you see
  • Your location
  • The masjid times you enter in the Android app
  • Your settings

If Android backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may keep a copy of this data in the backup in your own Google account. Your login is left out of that backup, and we have no access to it.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To sign you in and keep your account
  • To calculate prayer times for your area
  • To send Adhan, Iqamah and Dhikr reminders
  • To keep your Salah history and your streaks
  • To start your Salah history from the right day, using your date of birth
  • To show the scoreboard, if you join it
  • To answer your messages and fix problems you report
  • To tell you about the launch, if you joined the waitlist

4. Your Date of Birth

We ask for your date of birth when you create an account, for two reasons:

  • Your age — TAQWASTEPS is for people aged 13 and over. The app does not accept a date of birth that shows a younger age.
  • Your Salah history — The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Command your children to pray when they become seven years old." (Sunan Abi Dawud 495). TAQWASTEPS therefore counts your Salah history from the day you turned seven.

The age of seven is only where your own history begins. It does not mean that children of seven may hold an account.

Your date of birth is never shown to other people.

5. Tahajjud Reflections — Private Data

Your Tahajjud reflections are completely private. They are:

  • Kept only on your device, in storage that other apps cannot read
  • Never sent to our servers, so we cannot read them
  • Never shared with anyone
  • Never used for advertising or analytics

The only other copy that can exist is your own phone backup, as described in section 2.

6. Location Data

We use your location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.

  • The Android app asks for your approximate location, not your exact one. It reads it once when you set the app up, and again only when you ask it to. It does not follow you in the background.
  • Prayer times and the Qibla are worked out on your phone. Your location is kept on your phone and is not sent to our servers.
  • To show the name of your city, or to find a city you type, the app asks your phone's own location service. On most Android phones this is provided by Google.
  • The web app sends your position to the Aladhan prayer times service and to OpenStreetMap, to get prayer times and your city's name.

We do not track your movements, keep a history of where you have been, or store your location on our servers.

You can refuse or switch off location access in your device settings and choose your city by name instead.

7. Salah Protection (App Blocking)

If you switch on Salah protection, the Android app pauses social media and game apps at prayer times. To do this it needs three things, which you grant yourself in your phone's settings:

  • Usage access — to see which app is open on the screen during a prayer time
  • Display over other apps — to show the Salah screen on top of that app
  • The list of apps on your phone — to recognise which ones are social media or games

All of this happens on your phone. TAQWASTEPS does not keep a record of the apps you use and does not send this information to us or to anyone else. You can switch Salah protection off, or withdraw these permissions, at any time.

8. Notifications

We send notifications for:

  • A reminder before the Adhan (5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes before, as you choose)
  • Adhan time, and Iqamah time in the web app
  • Dhikr after Salah, with the morning and evening Adhkar
  • The Tahajjud alarm

In the Android app these are set on your phone and need no server. In the web app they are sent through your browser's notification service. You can manage notifications in the app settings or your device settings at any time.

9. The Scoreboard

The scoreboard is optional. Nothing about you appears on it unless you join.

  • If you join, other people see your name as it is on your account, your country and your streak numbers. They never see your email address or your date of birth.
  • You are given a code, such as TAQWA7K3M, to give to the friends you choose. It is made at random and says nothing about you.
  • You choose who sees you: only your friends, or everyone who is signed in. Someone becomes your friend only when one of you sends a request with the other's code and the other accepts it. A request you decline shows that person nothing.
  • You can change your name or country, or leave the scoreboard, at any time. Leaving removes your scoreboard entry, your code and your friend list.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. The services below are needed to run TAQWASTEPS and receive only what is described:

  • Supabase — our login service and database, where the account data in section 1 is stored. Its servers may be outside your country.
  • Google — if you sign in with Google; for city names through your phone's location service; and Google Play for payments, if Premium begins
  • EveryAyah — Quran recitation audio is fetched from everyayah.com when you play it. Like any website, it sees your internet address and which recitation was requested.
  • Aladhan and OpenStreetMap — prayer times and city names in the web app
  • Formspree — receives the waitlist and feedback forms on this website
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — deliver the fonts and code that this website and the web app need to load
  • When required by law

11. Data Security

We take data security seriously. Information travels between your device and our servers over an encrypted connection, and your login is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

12. Children's Privacy

TAQWASTEPS is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete the account and its data. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

13. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app or the web app: open Profile, choose Delete Account, and confirm with your email and password. Deleting cannot be undone.

In the Android app, everything is deleted at once:

  • Your login, your profile (name, email address and date of birth), your Salah log, your masjid times, your notification subscription, and your scoreboard entry, code, friends and friend requests
  • Everything TAQWASTEPS keeps for you on that phone, including your profile picture, and the reminders it had set

In the web app, your profile is removed at once and the data in that browser is erased at once; everything else we hold for you, including your login and your Salah log, is removed within 5–7 working days.

If you cannot open the app, write to taqwasteps@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we will delete it for you.

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. Waitlist email addresses are kept until the launch, or until you ask us to remove yours.

14. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Leave the scoreboard and withdraw any permission you gave the app
  • Opt out of email notifications
  • Request a copy of your data

To use any of these, contact us at the address below.
